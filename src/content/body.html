Research statement

I study how data and artificial intelligence can help organizations understand and anticipate customer behavior. My published research develops and tests a deep learning approach to predicting customer lifetime value in retail, and it checks the result with causal methods rather than relying on prediction accuracy alone.

My analytics projects apply the same thinking to customer churn, retention, marketing spend, and conversion funnels. I plan to continue this work on practical, evidence-based customer analytics that organizations can use to allocate resources and make better decisions.
